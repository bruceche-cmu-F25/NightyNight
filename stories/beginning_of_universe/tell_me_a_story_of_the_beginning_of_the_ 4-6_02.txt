A long, long time ago, there was a tiny seed. It was the smallest seed you can imagine. It was smaller than a speck of dust. It was smaller than a grain of sand. But this seed was very special.

Inside the seed, everything was tucked in tight. The bright sun was hiding inside. The white moon was resting there, too. All the twinkly stars were snuggled together. Even the blue sea and green trees were inside.

It was very warm in the seed. It felt like a big, soft hug. Everything was waiting for its turn to grow. The seed was warm and bright. It was the only thing in the dark. It was glowing with a soft light.

There was no sound at all. It was as quiet as a sleeping puppy. Everything was safe in the tiny seed. It was a very cozy place to be. You were there too. You were tucked away like a dream. The seed held the whole world in its heart. Think of that warm seed.

Soon, the tiny seed was ready to grow. It started to puff up. It grew bigger. Think of a happy balloon. Someone blows air into it. Puff. Puff. Puff. The balloon gets round and wide. It makes more room. There is so much space now. The space is warm and soft. Everything inside stretches out. It is like a big hug getting wider.

The balloon does not pop. It just keeps getting bigger. It makes a home for everything. The space is very quiet. It is very peaceful. The balloon is soft and slow. As it grows, it stays very calm. The pretty balloon is your home now. It is big. It is kind to you. You have a spot inside too. It is a cozy place to rest. Everything is light and airy. Now it is time to be still. The balloon holds you close. It keeps you warm in the dark. You are safe in the big, warm, soft space.

At first, the sky was very dark. It was big and quiet. Then, small lights started to wake up. These are the stars. One star twinkled. Then two stars twinkled. Soon, the whole sky was full of them. They are like a soft nightlight for the world. They glow with a warm light. They do not let the dark be too lonely.

The stars stay awake while you close your eyes. They watch over the hills and the trees. They watch over your house too. Each little light is a friend in the sky. They make the night feel safe and sweet. You can rest now. The stars are shining just for you. They keep the night bright for you. It is time to dream under the glow. You are safe in your warm bed. The stars will be your nightlight all through the dark. This nightlight is very big. It is over the whole sky. It is a gift for you. They shine so soft and kind. You are loved. Close your eyes now. Sleep tight, little one.

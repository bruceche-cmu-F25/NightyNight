Long ago, the sky was very dark. It was very, very quiet. There were no bright stars yet. There were no round moons. But there was a big blanket. It was a soft blanket of dust and mist. This blanket was very wide. It floated in the deep night. It did not have a sound. It did not have a light. The blanket was made of tiny bits. The bits were like soft dust in the air. They rested in the cold dark. The blanket was like a big, cozy bed. It waited for a long, long time. It was happy to be still. It was happy to be quiet. The blanket filled up the whole sky. It felt soft and thick. It was bigger than any house. It was bigger than any town. No wind moved the blanket. No toys played in the blanket. It just sat there in the dark. The blanket was very peaceful. It felt safe and warm. Everything was calm and sweet. The dark sky was a good place to rest. You are safe like that blanket too.

Then, the big blanket began to change. It started to curl up very tight. The blanket tucked in all its corners. It rolled into a big, round ball. This ball felt just like warm bread. It was soft and smooth. It was very cozy. The ball felt a little bit warm. It was like bread fresh from the oven. The warm ball grew tight and snug. It held onto itself like a hug. It was round and happy. The ball sat in the quiet dark. It felt safe in its new shape. It did not want to be spread out anymore. It wanted to be a round, soft loaf. The warm bread ball was thick. It was as puffy as a warm bun. Everything was quiet and still. The ball was ready for a nap. It was a happy, warm ball. Sleep now, little one, just like the bread. You are safe and warm in your bed.

The ball is very warm now. It is a big, soft ball. It lives in the deep, dark sky. Soon, something special happens. The warm ball starts to glow. It is not dark anymore. The ball looks like a friendly nightlight. The light is soft and gold. It is a kind and gentle light. It shines for the whole wide sky. It keeps the cold dark away. The new star is happy. It is just like the light in your room. It is a cozy and sweet light. It tells the sky it is time to rest. The star will stay bright and true. It will watch over the dark for you. You can see it from your soft bed. It is a little, happy spark. It is a big hug of golden light. The new star is born at last. It is time for you to dream now. The little star is here for you. Close your eyes now. The light is soft. You are safe too. Sleep tight, little one.

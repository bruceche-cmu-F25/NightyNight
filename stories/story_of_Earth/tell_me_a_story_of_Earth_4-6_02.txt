Long, long ago, the world was new. It was not cold. It was not hard like a rock. It was soft and very warm. Think of a loaf of bread. Think of bread right from the oven. The crust is gold. The inside is soft. It feels nice and warm on your hands. The world was like that bread. It was a big, warm ball. It glowed in the dark sky. It was as warm as a hug. All things were soft and cozy. The stars watched the warm world. The moon was not there yet. There were no green trees. There were no little birds. There was just the big, warm world. It sat in the quiet dark. It was happy and sleepy. It was like a fresh bun in a basket. The world was resting. It was getting ready. You can rest now, too. The big, warm world is safe. Close your eyes and feel the glow. You are safe and warm in your bed.

Then, the world was not hot anymore. It grew very quiet. The air grew dark and soft. The first drop fell. Plip. Plop. More drops came down. The rain fell on the hard ground. It fell for a long, long time. Tiny drops met each other. They made small puddles. The small puddles grew into big puddles. Soon, the whole world was one giant blue puddle. The water was deep. It was cool. The world was happy in its blue puddle. There was no more fire. There was no more smoke. All things were soft and wet. The blue water stayed very still. It felt safe and calm. The new water held the world tight. It was a sleepy blue world now. The stars looked down at the water. The water looked back at the stars. It was a good time to rest. The world was ready for a nap. You can rest now too. Sleep tight in your soft bed. You are safe and loved. The blue world is quiet for you. Sweet dreams, little one.

Now, the big Earth is like a soft garden. It has green grass and tall trees. The sun goes down to let the garden rest. The air gets cool and nice. The flowers close their petals for the night. They look like they are nodding their heads. The big Earth turns away from the light. It wants to take a long nap. All things are quiet and still. The bugs hide under soft leaves. The birds tuck their heads under their wings. The garden feels very safe. The garden is full of soft sounds. You can hear the wind in the grass. It makes a soft sound. It is time for the Earth to be dark. The dark is soft and blue. It covers the hills and the trees. It covers the sleepy flowers. The stars look down from way up high. They watch over the garden while it sleeps. You are safe in the garden too. The big Earth is resting with you. Your bed is soft and warm. Close your eyes now. Feel the cool air. Sleep well in our big, quiet home.

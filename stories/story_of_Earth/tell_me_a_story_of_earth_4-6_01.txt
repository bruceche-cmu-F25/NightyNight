Once upon a time, it was long ago. The world was new. It was not hard like a rock. It was not cold like snow. The world was a big, round ball. It was warm like bread from the oven. The world was soft and hot. It sat alone in the dark sky. It was not ready for trees yet. It was not ready for birds yet. It was just a warm, round place. It was as soft as dough in a bowl. The world spun slowly in the dark. It looked like a golden, glowing loaf. It was cozy like a fresh, hot bun. The world was a happy ball of warmth. It was a quiet and still place. Everything was soft and slow. You can feel that same warmth now. Close your eyes and think of the warm world. It is a good time to rest.

The world wanted to be cozy. It made a soft blue blanket. This blanket is made of light air. It wraps all the way around the round ball. The blanket stays close to the green trees. It stays close to the deep blue sea. You can feel it on your face. It feels like a gentle wind on your skin. The blue blanket is light and soft. It is clear to look through. But when the sun shines, it looks bright blue. It is a soft hug for the whole world. The blanket keeps the world nice and warm. It keeps the world safe. Birds fly inside this big blue blanket. Clouds float in it like bits of white fluff. We breathe the clean air from the blanket. It is always there for us. It never falls off or goes away. The world is tucked in tight. You are tucked in tight, too. The blue blanket is watching over the world.

The world is a big, soft, green garden now. It is a safe place for us to rest. This garden gives us everything we need. It has sweet air for us to breathe. It has fresh, cool water for us to drink. It has good food for us to eat. The garden is kind to us. Everything we need is right here. The tall trees stand still in the night. The flowers close their petals to sleep. The stars look down at the garden. They are like tiny nightlights in the sky. The bright moon glows white and soft. It watches over the birds in their nests. It watches over the bunnies in their holes. The round Earth is our own home. It is the best place to be. You are part of the garden, too. Now it is time to close your eyes. The garden is quiet and still. You are safe in your warm bed. Sleep well in our happy garden. Sleep well in our big, beautiful home.

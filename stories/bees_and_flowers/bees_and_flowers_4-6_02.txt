The sun is big and bright. It shines down on the green garden. The garden is a happy place. Flowers live in the garden. They have soft petals. They have green leaves. The flowers love the warm light. When the sun comes out, the flowers wake up. They stretch their leaves way up high. They reach for the gold light in the sky. The light helps the flowers grow tall. The more light they get, the bigger they get. They grow up, up, up. It is a good day to be a flower. The grass is soft. The air is warm. The garden is full of colors. Red flowers and blue flowers sit in the sun. They feel the warm sun on their faces. They are happy to be in the light. They stretch their leaves out wide. They are safe in the pretty garden. The garden is quiet and peaceful. Everything is slow and calm. Now it is time to rest in the warm sun. You are safe and warm too.

The fuzzy bee lands on a soft petal. The sun shines on the flower. Inside the flower is some yellow dust. This dust looks like tiny golden stars. The bee has a warm, fuzzy coat. The stars stick to her fuzzy coat. They cling to her fuzzy legs. They stay close to her soft belly. She wears the stars on her back. The stars go where the bee goes. The bee moves in a gentle way. She likes her golden stars. She keeps them very close. The dust does not fall off. It is light and quiet. The bee hums a low, sweet song. She carries her stars from place to place. The golden stars are safe on her fuzz. The golden stars belong on her coat. The wind blows softly through the garden. The bee is warm and happy. The golden stars shine in the light.

Now the sun goes down. The sky turns dark and blue. The flowers feel the cool air. They know it is time to rest. Each flower has soft petals. These petals are like a warm blanket. Slowly, the petals start to move. They fold up toward the middle. They tuck themselves in tight. The flowers hide their soft hearts inside. The garden is quiet now. No bees fly in the dark. The flowers do not need to stay open. They pull their blankets up high. They stay warm all through the night. The petals keep the cold away. The flowers are safe and sound. The moon looks down at the sleepy garden. Every little flower is tucked in. You are tucked in too. Your bed is soft and warm. It is time to close your eyes. Sleep well, little one.
